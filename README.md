# Adidas-US-Sales-Dashboard

## Objective

The objective of this report is to turn the raw, messy Adidas US sales export into a trustworthy, decision-ready view of how the business is performing.

## Dataset

9,500+ records

## Tools

-Power BI

-Excel

-Python

## KPIs

-Trend Analysis

-Product Performance

-Location Wise Performance

-Retailer Performance

**Overall Conclusion**

Adidas generated 899.90M in sales with a 36.91% profit margin, driven primarily by Men's Street Footwear and West Region/in-store sales. However, sales growth hasn't translated into proportional profit growth, signaling margin pressure — likely tied to over-reliance on a single product category and supply inconsistencies in March, June, and October. To sustain growth, Adidas should diversify its product and channel mix (reduce dependence on Men's Street Footwear and Foot Locker), address supply chain gaps in weak months, and investigate underperformance in the Midwest/Nebraska region.
